Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about StudySmart and learn how to use the application.

How do I create a study plan?

Go to the Subjects page, choose a subject, select a topic, choose your study time and click the Generate Plan button.

Where can I see my study plan?

Your study plan is automatically displayed on both the Calendar page and the Tasks page.

How do I mark a task as completed?

Open the Tasks page and click the Mark Complete button beside the task.

Why can't I see my study sessions?

Make sure you have generated a study plan. The sessions are saved in your browser and will appear in the Calendar and Tasks pages.

How do I delete a study plan?

Open the Calendar page and click the Clear All Plans button to remove all saved study sessions.

What does the Focus Timer do?

The Focus Timer helps you study using timed study sessions followed by short breaks to improve concentration.

Can I search for questions?

Yes. Use the search bar at the top of this page to quickly find a question.

Where is my study information saved?

Your study sessions are saved in your browser using localStorage, so they remain available when you revisit the application.
