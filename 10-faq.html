Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about study plans, tasks, the calendar and the focus timer.

Open the Subjects page, select a subject card, choose GCSE or A-Level, and then select a topic. Choose the study duration, preferred day and start date before pressing Generate Plan.

Your generated study sessions appear automatically on both the Calendar page and the Tasks page.

StudySmart creates one weekly session for each subtopic. The sessions are scheduled using the day and start date selected when creating the study plan.

Open the Calendar or Tasks page and press the Mark Complete button on the study session. Its completed status will be saved automatically.

Scheduled tasks have a future or current date. Pending tasks have passed their scheduled date but are not complete. Completed tasks have been marked as finished.

Open the Calendar page and press the red bin button on the session you want to remove. Confirm the message to delete it.

Press the Clear All Plans button at the top of the Calendar page. Confirm the message to remove all saved study sessions.

StudySmart keeps plans saved in your browser. New plans are added to existing plans until you delete individual sessions or use Clear All Plans.

Your study sessions are currently stored in your browser using localStorage. This means the information stays on the same browser and device.

The Focus Timer uses the Pomodoro method. It separates study time into focus sessions and short breaks to help you manage your concentration.
