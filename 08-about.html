About StudySmart

Helping Students Stay Organised and Succeed

StudySmart is a web-based study planner designed to help students organise their subjects, manage assignments, schedule study sessions, and improve productivity. Our goal is to make studying easier by providing simple tools that keep everything in one place.

Our Mission

To help students stay organised, reduce stress, and achieve their academic goals through smart planning and effective time management.

Our Vision

To become a trusted study planning platform that supports students throughout their educational journey.

Why Choose StudySmart?

Manage Subjects

Study Calendar

Task Manager

Create tasks, monitor progress, and stay on top of deadlines.

Focus Timer
