About StudySmart

Helping Students Stay Organised and Succeed

StudySmart is a web-based study planner created to help students organise their learning, manage assignments, track progress and improve study habits. It provides an easy-to-use dashboard, calendar, task manager, focus timer and study planner to help students achieve their academic goals.

Get Started Learn More

Our Mission

Our mission is to help every student develop better study habits through planning, organisation and time management.

Our Vision

We aim to make studying less stressful by providing one place to organise all academic activities.

Who It's For

StudySmart is designed for GCSE, A-Level, college and university students who want to improve productivity.

What StudySmart Offers

Study Calendar

Organise your study schedule with a clear calendar view.

Task Manager

Keep track of assignments and completed work.

Subject Planner

Create personalised study plans for each subject.

Focus Timer

Stay productive using the Pomodoro technique.
